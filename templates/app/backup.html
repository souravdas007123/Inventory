Automatic daily backup (PythonAnywhere)

  1. Open the Tasks tab in PythonAnywhere.
  2. Choose a daily time (for example, late at night) and enter this command:
    ~/.virtualenvs/inv/bin/python ~/Inventory/manage.py daily_tasks

This one command takes the backup and then emails you the day's summary (sales, credit, purchases and stock alerts) to the email in Shop Settings. If you only want the backup, use backup_db instead of daily_tasks.

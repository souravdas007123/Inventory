Everything a medical store needs every day

Built around how a pharmacy really works: strips and tablets, batches that expire, suppliers whose prices change.

🧾

Fast GST billing

Scan or search, Enter for the next field, F9 to save. Rate is filled from the MRP, GST is worked out for you, and credit, UPI and cash are all supported.

⏳

Batch and expiry (FEFO)

The oldest valid batch is sold first. Expired stock is never billed, and you see what is about to expire before it becomes a loss.

🔁

Alternative medicines

Out of stock? Billing suggests another medicine with the same composition that you have in stock, cheapest first.

⚖️

Supplier price comparison

See who charged what, who is cheapest now, and get an alert when a price jumps. Purchase orders by print, email or WhatsApp.

📈

Smart reordering

From your real sales it shows how many days stock will last and how much to order, in whole packs.

💊

Schedule H, H1 and X register

Doctor and patient details are asked at billing for scheduled drugs. The register exports to Excel for inspection.

🧮

GST and accountant export

GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and HSN summary, plus one Excel with sales, purchases, credit and debit notes, expenses and payments for your CA.

📥

Import from Excel

Bring in products, stock and purchases from Excel or CSV. You see a preview and any problems before anything is saved.

⏸️

Hold and resume bills

A customer is still choosing? Hold the bill, serve the next one, and pick it up again later.

Questions shop owners ask

Short answers. Ask us anything else.

Where is my data stored?

In your own database on your own server. Nothing is shared with anyone, and a compressed backup is taken every day.

Do I need special hardware?

No. MedSync runs in a web browser on any computer, tablet or phone. A barcode scanner works as a keyboard.

Can my accountant use it?

Yes. The accountant export gives sales and purchase registers, credit and debit notes, GST by rate, expenses and payments in one Excel file.

Is Schedule H and H1 selling tracked?

Yes. Billing asks for doctor and patient details for scheduled drugs and the drug register can be exported to Excel.

Can I move my existing stock into MedSync?

Yes. Products, stock and purchases can be imported from Excel or CSV with a preview before anything is saved.

A day in your shop, with MedSync

It follows the way a chemist's day really goes, from the first look at the shelves to the last backup.

  1. Open the shopThe dashboard shows low stock, what expires this month and any supplier price that jumped.
  2. Serve customersScan or search, bill, print. Credit, cash, UPI or card, with scheduled drugs handled as the law asks.
  3. Receive stockEnter the supplier bill. Warnings catch duplicate invoices and odd prices before they reach your books.
  4. Close the dayA summary of sales by payment mode, credit given and collected, and top sellers, ready to share on WhatsApp.
  5. While you sleepThe daily backup runs, and the health score is measured again.

What it does, in detail

Every item here is already in the application. Nothing on this page is a promise for later.

Billing

Fast, correct bills at the counter

  • Scan or type. Search by name or barcode, Enter moves on, F9 saves.
  • The rate fills itself. From the MRP, for the unit you pick (strip or tablet), with GST worked out. A rate you type is never overwritten.
  • Oldest batch first (FEFO). Expired stock cannot be billed.
  • Out of stock? It suggests medicines with the same composition that you have, cheapest first, and replaces the row in one tap.
  • Hold and resume. Put a bill aside while the customer decides, and finish it later.
  • Customers and credit. Type a phone number to pick the customer and see the credit due against the limit. Partial payments and a ledger per customer.

Stock and expiry

Know what will expire before it costs you

  • Every purchase makes a batch with its expiry, so stock is tracked batch by batch.
  • Expiry page lists what expires soon, so a return to the supplier is one click away.
  • Returns and write-offs. Sales return, purchase return and expired or damaged write-off. Stock, accounts and profit adjust by themselves.
  • Product preview. See a product's batches, prices and history without opening the edit form.
  • Stock count. Compare shelf counts with the system and correct the difference.

Accounts and compliance

Your CA gets one file. The inspector gets a clean register.

Trust

A health score that is measured, not guessed

The owner sees a score out of 100 on the admin home. Each part comes from a real measurement, every lost point has a reason, and a check that cannot run is never counted as good.

  • Data (40): stock against batches, negative batches.
  • Safety (25): backup age, whether the latest backup really opens, database integrity, secure settings.
  • Speed (20): pages timed on the server.
  • Shop (15): expired stock, low stock, missing MRP and HSN.

Built so mistakes get caught early

A pharmacy loses money in small errors. These checks run on their own.

  • Duplicate supplier invoice is refused, in any letter case.
  • Odd purchase entries are flagged: price 40% above or below the usual, a quantity ten times the usual, a price of zero, a selling price below cost, a missing or short expiry.
  • Expired batches can never be billed.
  • Scheduled drugs cannot be billed without the doctor and patient details.
  • Stock and batches are compared every time the health score is measured.
  • Imports show a preview and mark which columns are needed before anything is saved.
  • Warnings never block you. A deliberate unusual entry is saved with one click.
  • An audit log records important changes and who made them.
